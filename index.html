Export to ATAK

In ATAK: Import Manager → Local SD → pick the .zip (or .kml). Segments arrive as labeled, team-colored polygons grouped by team, with the LKP marked. Send the .zip as a data package to push it to every device.

Category distances are approximate starting values for a temperate, flat-terrain region (straight-line distance from the IPP). Confirm against your Lost Person Behavior reference and adjust for terrain, weather and subject history. POA: the area inside the 25% ring holds 25%, split by area. Squares take their share from the ring model behind them: 25% between the 25% ring and the 50% distance, 25% from 50–75%, 20% from 75–95%, and 5% beyond, spread by area.
